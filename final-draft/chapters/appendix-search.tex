\section{Notes}

Search fields: Web of Science Topic (title, abstract, author keywords, Keywords Plus); Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY (title, abstract, keywords); Policy Commons title and summary fields. Scopus searches were limited to decision sciences, social sciences, business, economics, arts and humanities, and multidisciplinary subject areas; to articles, book chapters, and reviews; and to English-language records. Search strings were calibrated through the scoping iterations described in \S\ref{sec:search-strategy}, and the disclosed deviations from the formative protocol (including the Overton substitution) are documented in \S 3.3.1 and \S 3.6.
