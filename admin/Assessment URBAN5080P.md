# Research Questions + Concepts

### Concepts
- **The Advocacy Coalition Framework (ACF)** is ==a theory that explains how policy change occurs when individuals and organisations with similar policy beliefs work together==. The ACF can be used to understand policy processes, along with the multiple streams framework and policy cycle models of analysis.
- **The Multiple Streams Framework (MSF)** is ==a method for analysing policymaking that's based on the idea that three distinct but connected streams influence the process:==
	- **Problem stream**; Actors like the media, policy communities, and the public bring attention to situations that need policy intervention.
	- ​**Policy stream**; Policymakers, analysts, and experts propose solutions, considering their feasibility, effectiveness, and resource needs.
	- **Politics stream**; This stream considers the political climate and public opinion surrounding an issue.
